| Premium | 118.8% | 99.1% | -19.7pp |

**Conclusion:** Fire impact contained at ~8% valuation reduction. Still well above DownUnder's $650mm floor.

## Task 4: Provide an Investment Recommendation

**Objective:** Email to WorldWide Brewing team with key considerations and revised valuation recommendation.

**Files:**
- `Task 4 - Article v3.pdf` - News article on competitive bidding
- `Task 4 - Template - Revised.pptx` - Email template
- `NBA Happy Hour Co - DCF model - Task 4 - Revised Template.xlsx` - DCF model
- `Task4_Email_Recommendation_Final.pptx` - **FINAL OUTPUT** - Investment recommendation email

**Key Considerations:**

**1. Offsetting Factors for Fire Impact:**
- One-time operational disruption (not structural impairment)
- Long-term 1% terminal growth trajectory intact
- NWC release in FY21 partially cushions EBITDA decline
- Competitive bidding (DownUnder >$650mm + 2 PE firms + 1 US brewer) validates non-material long-term impact

**2. Materiality Assessment:**
- **Short-term:** Significant (FY21 EBITDA negative at -$9.8mm)
- **Medium-term:** Moderate (FY22+ recovery to $112mm+ EBITDA)
- **Long-term:** Limited (terminal value 49% of NPV largely unaffected; only 8% valuation impact)

**3. Credibility of Competitive Bid Quantum:**
- DownUnder's $650mm+ valuation provides credible floor (~12% discount to revised $738.5mm EV)
- Multiple interested parties validate process integrity
- Binding offer deadline (Friday) and Q3 close timeline suggest serious, well-informed bidders

**Revised Valuation vs. Preliminary:**
- Preliminary: $803mm EV | 361c | 118.8% premium
- Revised: $738.5mm EV | 328.6c | 99.1% premium
- Change: -$64.5mm (-8%) | -32.4c | -19.7pp

**Recommendation:** Revised valuation of $738.5mm EV (328.6c/share, 99% premium) remains robust and well above the credible competitive floor of $650mm. **Proceed with due diligence and prepare a competitive response.**
